Example week: SQL homework due Wed 11:59 pm, Stats midterm Thu 9 am, DB final next Mon. Study times are weekdays 6–10 pm, and there's a Canvas study group Wed 8–9 pm.

1. How should the week show study blocks? (click A or B)

A

Day list: today's Week screen, with blocks added

Each day card lists its study blocks by time, then its deadlines. Dashed = suggested (tap ✓ or ✕), filled = accepted. Easy to read on a phone; keeps the screen you already have.

B

Time grid: a calendar, three days at a time

Hours down the side, blocks drawn at their times, deadlines along the top. Shows free time and clashes at a glance, but only three days fit, and it's a new screen to build.
